# Outfit art references - CleanCity 0.6

Checked 9 October 2026. These are public-domain (CC0) visual references. New outfits are original Canvas drawings in `lib/game/profession_art.dart`, integrated with the existing hero face atlas and walking animation; the downloaded pixel sprites are not shipped in the game.

- Chasersgaming, [NPC Characters (20)](https://opengameart.org/content/npc-characters-20), CC0. Reference for firefighter, doctor, chef, astronaut and mechanic profession silhouettes, headgear and equipment. Source page and sprite sheet retained in `docs/references/v6/npc-characters-source.html` and `npc-characters-cc0.png`.
- Kenney, [Roguelike Characters](https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-characters), CC0. Reference for fantasy costume construction and alchemist accessories. Source page, preview, source archive and packaged license retained in `docs/references/v6/`. See `kenney-license.txt` for the source license and CC0 link.

Credits are also visible in Settings. No Marvel, League of Legends or other third-party franchise costumes were added. These references do not change the provenance of the existing six outfits or face atlas.

New stable catalog IDs: 6 Firefighter (12,000 coins), 7 Alchemist (55 gems), 8 Doctor (15,000 coins), 9 City chef (18,000 coins), 10 Astronaut (70 gems), 11 Mechanic (22,000 coins). All are available without purchases in the isolated test build.
