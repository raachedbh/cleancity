# Architecture references for CleanCity 0.5

These are original vector interpretations adapted to CleanCity's angled cartoon view and gameplay scale, not scans or one-to-one reconstructions. Google Maps links for Tunis, Florence, Paris and Kyoto were attempted on 9 October 2026, but the interactive maps could not be retrieved by the browsing tool. The following publicly accessible references were used instead.

- **Tunis Avenue:** Municipal Theatre on Avenue Habib Bourguiba. Curved cream Art Nouveau facade, three arched upper windows, balcony, three entry doors and decorative relief. [INTERFERENCE Tunis, City Theater](https://2022.intunis.net/city-theater/) and [Tunisair La Gazelle 61, printed pages 28-29 / PDF spread 14](https://www.tunisair.com/sites/default/files/2025-09/gazelle61.pdf). The avenue's clock tower remains elsewhere on the map. Other street buildings have pale plaster, balconies and teal shutters.
- **Porto Sole / Italy:** The Arcone of Piazza della Repubblica, Florence, with a large central vault, paired pilasters, cornices and balustrades. [Comune di Firenze / FeelFlorence](https://www.feelflorence.it/it/punti-di-interesse/piazza-della-repubblica). Other blocks use warm masonry, terracotta roofs, shutters and ground-floor shops.
- **Paris Gardens:** Place des Vosges, red brick with pale stone chains, arcades, steep slate roofs, dormers and chimneys. [Paris je t'aime, official tourism office](https://parisjetaime.com/eng/transport/place-des-vosges-p1907). Address-based height and color variants mix with the existing conservatory and other shops.
- **Kyoto Lanterns:** Gion machiya streets, with low timber facades, close latticework, bamboo blinds, tiled eaves, shop curtains and lanterns. [Japan National Tourism Organization: Gion](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/84/). The existing shrine and garden landmarks remain.

The downloaded reference photos/PDF are only in `docs/references/` for local review; none is bundled into the APK. All new building drawing code is in `lib/game/reference_architecture.dart`. The renderer records each lot once, culls off-screen buildings, and sorts them around pedestrians and the player. Daytime maps receive a subtle evening tint in dark mode; the already-dark Midnight map is not dimmed further.
